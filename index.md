## Selected work

### Peyk

A proactive personal assistant on Telegram that watches email, calendars and documents, prioritizes what matters, and drafts outgoing messages for human approval. A deterministic interruption budget governs when it can notify the user. Peyk was co-built with Mehmet Ekin Derin.

- Role: Co-creator and developer
- Stack: Python, FastAPI, Strands Agents, Amazon Bedrock, Claude, Composio, PostgreSQL, pgvector, Neon and Fly.io
- Website: <https://peyk.chat>
- Source: <https://github.com/akcware/peyk>
- Project story: <https://devpost.com/software/peyk-knocks-only-when-it-matters>

### Tesseract Go

An iOS learning app that turns photographed or written STEM questions into animated explanations. It supports seven languages and uses a retrieval pipeline with pgvector and HNSW hybrid search plus a Python and Manim rendering service.

- Role: Founder and developer, 2025 to present
- Stack: iOS, RAG, pgvector, HNSW, Python, Manim, Azure and Hetzner
- App Store: <https://apps.apple.com/de/app/tesseract-ki-mathe-assistent/id6776019680>
